## 1. GAMEPLAY

### i. GAME STRUCTURE

Our Game is a skill-based chore game where players must accomplish X-amount of tasks under a time limit. Each task should take 5-10 seconds challenging the player to quickly identify tools and their connected jobs.

### ii. GAME OBJECTIVE

Players will be pitted against each other competing for items to complete their quests. Tasks the players will perform include;

- Using an axe to cut trees

- Mining Ore/Rock spots

- Using a scythe on weeds

- Digging up mushrooms

- Tilling soil

### iii. PLAYERS

This game is multiplayer, allowing for 2 players to face off. Both players will compete in the same game arena.

### iv. OBJECT TYPES

Grabbable Objects:

-          Axe

-          Pick-axe

-          Shovel

-          Tilling Hoe

Destructible Objects:

-          Trees

-          Rocks

-          Weeds

-          Mushrooms

### v. CONTROLS

- 8-way Joystick for movement

- 2 Buttons (Primary Action - Pickup/Drop  / Secondary Action - Use Tool)


### vi. GAMEPLAY LOOP

Players will be given a task and compete to finish it first. For example “Cut 8 trees”. On completing a task, their next task will generate going through all 4 tasks. The first player to complete all tasks wins the game.

### vii. SCORE SYSTEM

Score is relevant to the amount of tasks/actions in task completed times current game time.

### viii. GAME SYSTEM

Arcade Cabinet

## 2.  OBJECTIVE STATEMENT

The players should feel frenzied excitement while trying to out-speed their opponent.

Overall, we want our gameplay to be;

-          Fast-paced

-          Instinctual

-          And based around time management

The player should be forced to react quickly and efficiently with little time to complete their tasks. The player will have simplified mechanics allotting for the gameplay to spark quick thinking, and instinctual action.

## 3. DESIGN RATIONAL

### i. DESIGN STRUCTURE

Since many of our players may not be as experienced in gaming we want to create a game with simplified logic focused around time completion.

- Firstly, a clock counting down even outside of games is intuitive to a time crunch and should prompt players to complete tasks within the timeframe.

- Secondly all tasks should be easy to pair with their tool. Such as an axe for cutting trees, It should be easily identifiable to the participant allowing them to focus on speed over thought.

### ii. PROTOTYPING GOALS

After prototyping we will be aware of how the core mechanics of our game are interfaced by players, allowing our company to move forward with more structure and confidence in the game. This method also allows the company to avoid unnecessary costs if the game turns out to not be fun.

### iii. TARGET AUDIENCE

This game is designed for all ages 6+ towards the generic public. Overall this game should provide an easily accessible experience for audiences to be introduced to the capabilities of Mohawks Game Design Program.

## 4. LEVEL RATIONAL

### i. LEVEL STRUCTURE

![[studio-wide_level-process-work.jpeg|231]]![[LEVEL.png|329]]

Each player will have a base shed on either side of the map, this will be where they spawn. Within the field tools and destructible objects will spawn prompting players to assess the entire field to figure out the fastest way to outplay their opponent. The full field will always be in view to account for both players  vision, and to let players always know the location of their objectives since speed is the goal. The overall background (grass) and fence is simplified to further allow emphasis on the spawned objects for visual digestion.

## 6. STORY

Welcome to Moonpoint Meadows! A bustling village looking to cultivate its farm lands. You have been invited by the Mayor to join his recultivation initiative, offering varying farmers homes and lands of plot in order to boost the village’s economic exports. There’s only 1 catch: each farm plot is shared by 2 farm initiates, and who so happens to be sharing your plot with you? None other than your arch nemesis since birth!!! Ahead of you lies a battle of wits, speed, and endless willpower as you strive to out cultivate your rival!
